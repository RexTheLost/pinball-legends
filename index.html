PINBALL LEGENDS

ARCADE ARENA

3D physics pinball where your ball is a gladiator. Newest build: v0.6.1 (early prototype)

Windows (64-bit)

Unzip and run PinballLegends.exe. No install. Keyboard or controller.

Android (phone)

Install the APK (allow installs from your browser). Plays upright: hold the left / right side of the screen for the flippers, tap abilities, or use a controller.

In this build

  • Two layers: score enough on the town playfield and a sinkhole opens. Drop into the dungeon below, clear 3 waves, beat the boss and smash open its chest for class gear, XP and gold, then pop back up to town.
  • Five classes (Striker, Guardian, Trickster, Alchemist, Gunslinger), each with a passive and four abilities.
  • Prize counter: every game earns tickets to trade for weapons, armor and ball skins. Gold upgrades your gear.
  • Quest VR and an in-game update notice when a new build is out.
  • Full table: two ramps, drop targets, standups, a spinner and outlanes.
  • Sound and music, sparks and screen shake, ball trails, flipper skins and table themes.
  • Daily and weekly challenges for extra tickets and gold.
  • Side wings: full-size Forge and Treasury tables bolted onto the cabinet, joined by rails.
  • Deeper dungeon: four more floors (Crypt, Lava Caverns, Frozen Abyss, Demon Throne), each with its own boss and hazards.
  • Workshop table upgrades, one-game boosts, and real multiball.
  • PACHI-QUEST pachinko in the arcade: jackpots give you a free Multiball Starter for pinball.
  • Options with master, music and effects volume.
  • A full 3D cabinet in a neon arcade: flippers, plunger, pop bumpers, slingshots, rollover lanes, and a plastic ramp with a wire return.
  • Enemies roam the playfield in waves: Slimes, Imps and Golems. Hit them hard to kill them for points, XP and coins.
  • Striker class: stronger flippers and harder hits. Ability Power Slam supercharges your flippers and doubles damage for 6 seconds.
  • Controller support with analog plunger and rumble. Local high scores.
